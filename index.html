I am an English language educator, teacher trainer, and researcher working at the intersection of second language acquisition, higher education, and artificial intelligence. I help educators and students use AI intelligently, ethically, and independently in academic learning.

My work includes professional development for university faculty, AI literacy workshops for students, and research on how AI is changing language teaching, learning, feedback, and assessment. I focus on practical approaches that strengthen pedagogy, academic integrity, learner agency, and human-centered education.
