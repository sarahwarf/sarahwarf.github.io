About

I am an English language educator, teacher trainer, and researcher working at the intersection of language education, higher education, and AI literacy.

I am currently a Senior Lecturer in Academic English at NYU Shanghai, where I teach and design courses for multilingual university students. I am also affiliated faculty with the NYU Shanghai Data Science Center, contributing to interdisciplinary work on AI literacy, academic English, and technology-mediated learning.

I am completing a PhD in Education at Pädagogische Hochschule Heidelberg in Germany. My research examines how university faculty understand and adopt AI in their teaching, with particular attention to STEM and English-medium instruction contexts.

Experience and Focus

I have worked in Chinese universities since 2016 and in university English-language education for more than a decade. My work includes academic English, writing, reading, assessment, curriculum development, faculty development, and AI-informed pedagogy.

I am an English Language Specialist with the U.S. Department of State English Language Specialist Program. Through this work, I have designed and delivered teacher education and professional-development projects in China, Mexico, Brazil, Russia, and across the East Asia Pacific region.

My recent funded projects include research on AI readiness among university faculty, AI use in STEM English-medium instruction, and AI-supported academic English learning. I have published and presented on generative AI, academic writing, oral assessment, critical AI literacy, and language teaching at international conferences and in professional publications.
