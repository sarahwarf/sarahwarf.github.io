About

I currently teach academic English at NYU Shanghai and am completing a PhD in Education at Pädagogische Hochschule Heidelberg (Germany), where my research examines how university faculty understand and adopt AI in their teaching.

Experience and Focus

  • Nearly a decade working in Chinese universities in English language teaching, collaborating with faculty on classroom practice and curriculum development
  • Senior Lecturer in Academic English at NYU Shanghai since 2019
  • Extensive experience supporting undergraduate and postgraduate students in English-medium academic contexts
  • Regular invited speaker and presenter at international conferences on English language teaching, higher education, and AI in education
  • Researcher and writer on language education and higher education pedagogy, including the integration of AI into teaching and learning
