AI Literacy for Students and Families

I offer student-facing talks on practical AI literacy for academic learning, English-language development, and responsible technology use. These talks draw on my work with university educators and language programs, but are designed for non-specialist audiences: students, parents, and families who want clear, practical guidance.

For Students

Using AI for studying, writing, research, presentations, English learning, and independent academic work.

For Parents and Families

Understanding how students are using AI, what responsible use looks like, and how to support learning without panic or over-policing.

For Student Support Programs

Guest talks for orientation, academic skills programs, writing centers, advising teams, and career-readiness initiatives.

Format

Talks are 60–90 minutes and delivered from existing material, with light audience interaction and Q&A. They are designed as keynote-style guest lectures rather than customized workshops.

Ways to Work Together

Guest Lecture

A single, ready-to-deliver talk for a school, program, or event — chosen from an existing emphasis such as academic work and study, career readiness, or critical thinking and responsible AI use. Best for orientation sessions, assemblies, and one-off programming.

Individual Consultation

For a student or family seeking more individualized guidance, I offer a one-time consultation: a focused look at a student's current AI use, English communication, and academic goals, with practical recommendations. This can stand on its own or serve as the entry point into an ongoing coaching package for students who want continued support.

Custom Workshop or Series

For programs and institutions that want more than a guest lecture, I offer tailored workshops and workshop series built around your group's specific needs, with advance planning and interactive activities. This is a separate, more involved offering from the standard guest lecture.
