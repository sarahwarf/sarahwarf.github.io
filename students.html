For Students

I offer practical training in using AI responsibly for academic writing, English-language learning, research, presentations, study skills, and independent learning. My goal is to help you use AI as a tool that strengthens your own thinking and language skills — not one that replaces them.

This work grows out of my teaching and research at NYU Shanghai, where I focus on helping students build independent AI literacy skills alongside strong academic English and critical thinking abilities.

Areas of focus

  • Using AI responsibly and effectively in academic writing, from brainstorming through revision
  • Building independent English-language learning habits that AI can support but not replace
  • Applying AI tools to research, keyword searches, and source evaluation
  • Preparing presentations and study materials with AI as a support tool
  • Developing critical thinking skills for evaluating AI-generated content
