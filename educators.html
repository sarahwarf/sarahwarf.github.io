For Educators and Institutions

I work with university faculty and language programs on AI-informed teaching, assessment, feedback, and academic English. My professional development focuses on practical strategies that instructors can apply directly in their classrooms while keeping pedagogy — not the tools themselves — at the center.

I offer workshops and invited talks that can be delivered as individual sessions, short series, or full professional development programs, either online or in person, and adapted to your institutional context.

What this includes

  • Workshops and talks on AI-informed language teaching, assessment, and feedback
  • Curriculum support for EFL, EMI, and academic English programs
  • Peer observation and reflective practice frameworks for AI-integrated teaching
  • Research-informed guidance on evaluating and using AI tools responsibly in the classroom
