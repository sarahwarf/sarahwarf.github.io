It was a pleasure to return to Beijing, and the Beijing American Center for the first time in almost three years. I also enjoy working with the team in Beijing, reconnecting with old friends, and meeting new local teachers. For this all-day workshop, I shared my recent work I've been doing with teachers who want to incorporate AI into their practice. We covered AI Quality Checklists, to ensure that the materials we create are a good fit for our practice and learners and discussed building AI agents through a sharing out of a course assistant I'm using this fall at NYU Shanghai. Finally, we wrapped up the day going over humanistic teaching approaches to keep in mind as we continue teaching in AI-forward classrooms. It was inspiring to see all the enthusiasm, critical thinking, and collaboration in the room. Looking forward to working with all of you in the upcoming academic year.
